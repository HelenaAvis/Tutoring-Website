My Services

I offer online sessions and in-person sessions around the Uckfield area.

My first session is a free 30 minute introductory session, which is aimed at getting to know the student's goals and learning style. This allows me to tailor sessions to best suit each student's individual needs.

After the introductory session, I offer individual 1 hour sessions at a rate of £20 per hour. I also offer a bundle of 4 sessions for a discounted price of £60.

If you are interested in booking a session, please contact me to enquire about any further information, or to book an introductory session.
