About Me

Hi! I’m Helena, a tutor of GCSE and A-Level Maths and Computer Science.

I studied Maths and Computer Science at GCSE and A-Level. After I finished my A-Levels, I went on to complete an MEng Computer Science degree at the University of Southampton.

My lessons are shaped by each student’s individual needs and I believe in adapting my teaching style to suit the way each student feels they learn the most effectively. Please check out the services I offer, and contact me if you are interested!
